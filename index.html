Two ways to work with us

Full representation

We handle the whole application and every message with the Home Office.

  • Advice on eligibility and documents
  • We complete and submit the application for you
  • Covering letter and legal representations
  • We deal with UKVI until a decision arrives

Full document check

You prepare the application yourself; we check it before you pay the Home Office fees.

  • A line-by-line review of the form and evidence
  • Missing or wrongly formatted evidence flagged
  • A covering letter that sets out how you meet the rules
  • Costs less than full representation

For partner, points-based, ILR and citizenship applications.

Fixed fees where available. The first consultation is free and we usually reply within 1 working day.
