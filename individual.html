We go through your form and evidence before you submit to UK Visas and Immigration. If an answer could confuse the caseworker, or evidence is missing or in the wrong format, we tell you what to change. We also draft a covering letter showing how you meet the rules.

Changes to settlement

On 2 November 2026 the government confirmed a 10-year baseline for most routes, with 5 years kept for partners of British citizens and BN(O) holders. Rules are expected from December 2026. Until they are in force, today’s qualifying periods apply.
