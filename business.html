Sponsor licences and business immigration

Sponsorship is a major part of our practice. We help UK employers get and keep their licence, and help founders and overseas companies set up here.

For employers

  • Sponsor licence applications
  • Skilled Worker visas and extensions
  • Recruiting from outside the UK
  • Preventing illegal working: right to work checks
  • Compliance support and preparing for Home Office audits
  • Temporary Worker routes

For founders and overseas companies

  • Innovator Founder visas
  • Global Business Mobility: Senior or Specialist Worker, Graduate Trainee, UK Expansion Worker, Service Supplier, Secondment Worker
  • Business plans written for the immigration rules

Closed routes: extensions and settlement only

Tier 1 Investor, Tier 1 Entrepreneur, Start-up and Sole Representative of an Overseas Business are closed to new applicants. If you are already on one of them, we advise on extensions and settlement.
